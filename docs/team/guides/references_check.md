## Why this matters

B6 marks referencing and integrity. A reference that doesn't exist, or a wrong DOI, looks like an AI invention
and costs far more than the time it takes to check. Every reference in the paper must be real and correct.

## Step 2: check each one (about 5 min each)

1. Go to <https://search.crossref.org> and paste the title.
2. Check that the authors, year, journal and DOI match what we wrote. Copy the DOI exactly.
3. If Crossref has nothing (standards, reports, websites), find it on the publisher's own site (ISO, NIOSH,
   Copernicus) and record the URL and the date you checked it.
4. Open the DOI link (`https://doi.org/<DOI>`) and make sure it goes to the right paper.
5. Check that the paper actually says what we cite it for. Read the abstract at least.

## Step 3: format (20 min)

- IEEE style, numbered in the order they first appear in the text.
- Every number in the text has an entry, and every entry is cited in the text at least once.
- Same format for every entry: initials before surname, title in quotes, journal in italics, vol., no., pp., year, doi.
