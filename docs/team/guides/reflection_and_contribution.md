# Reflection, contribution and AI sign-off (Everyone, Week 10)

**Owner:** each member for their own · **Due:** Thu 26 Nov · **Time:** about 1.5 hours

Each member writes their own. Nobody else can write it for you, and AI must not write it for you.

## Part 1: contribution row (10 min)

Open [`CONTRIBUTIONS.md`](../../../CONTRIBUTIONS.md) and update **your own row only**:
- What you did in Weeks 8–11 (one or two lines, specific: "wrote Section VII", "ran the Nielsen review").
- Your commit hashes. Find them on GitHub: repository → Commits → click your commit → copy the 7-character code.
- Commit the change from **your own account** ([how](how_to_submit.md)).

## Part 2: individual reflection (about 1 hour; check the brief for the word limit)

Answer these in your own words:
1. **What I did.** My tasks and what came out of them.
2. **What I learned.** One technical thing (for example how event-based scoring works) and one teamwork thing.
3. **What went wrong and what I did about it.** One honest example; how it was fixed or what I'd do differently.
4. **How I used AI.** Which tools, for what, what I checked or changed. Must match the GenAI log.
5. **What I'd do next** if the project continued.

Tip: write about your own work. Do not comment on other members' effort.

## Part 3: AI sign-off for your part (20 min)

Most code in this repo was written with Claude Code and declared in [`docs/genai_log.md`](../../genai_log.md).
The panel may ask any of us to explain a part. Tick these for the part in your contribution row:

- [ ] I can explain what every function in my part does and why
- [ ] I ran the tests and the pipeline myself (or watched Rayen run them and can describe the output)
- [ ] I checked at least one output by hand (for example one WBGT limit, one lead time)
- [ ] I recorded anything I changed or rejected in `docs/iteration_log.md`

The [Extensive guide](https://claude.ai/artifact/FJjrKEVS8qwHhH1h1tCE1T) explains every part in plain words.
